Case Study 02

Food Processing Leader — ¥6M Contract

Food Processing Leader — ¥6M Contract

Client Background

A nationally recognized agricultural industrialization leader with annual revenue exceeding ¥5 billion, exporting products to over 30 countries and regions.

Core Challenge

HACCP/ISO22000 food safety system demands extremely strict lubrication control. Traditional lubrication methods could not precisely meter food-grade lubricant, creating contamination risks from over-lubrication and resulting in a product pass rate of only 97.2%.

Solution

  • Full production line digital lubrication retrofit covering filling, sealing, and packaging critical stations
  • P02 micro-flow metering cartridge delivering 0.02mL/min precision lubrication, eliminating over-lubrication
  • Fully sealed IP67 design meeting cleanroom hygiene requirements
  • Complete data logging per lubrication point enabling full-chain traceability from raw materials to finished products
